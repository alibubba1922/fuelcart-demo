# FuelCart grocery demo

Browser demo of barcode-assisted grocery carts and nutrition/healthier-swap comparisons. FuelCart is a placeholder name. App pricing shown is $4.99 one-time, with no subscription or in-app purchases.

## Demo status
Not verified for real-phone or in-store use. Retail prices, nutrition, swaps and barcodes may be estimates or unverified; check in-app labels and actual shelf prices. Tax handling needs review. Camera scanning needs HTTPS and permission. No medical advice or weight-loss promises.
