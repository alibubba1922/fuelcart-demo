    🏋️ Training fuel

    Before (1–3 h): carbs + some protein, low fat/fibre — e.g. oats with banana, toast with PB, yogurt + fruit.

    During (60 min+ or hot): water; a sports drink only for long or intense sessions.

    After (within ~2 h): about 20–40 g protein with carbs — e.g. a protein shake + fruit, chocolate milk, chicken + rice.

    All day: spread protein over 3–5 meals; mass gainers are a convenience, not a requirement; energy drinks are caffeine + sugar, not fuel.
